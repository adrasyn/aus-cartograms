- **ABS ASGS2021 State and Territory**, GDA2020: dedicated land/coast outlines. [Official archive](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/digital-boundary-files/STE_2021_AUST_SHP_GDA2020.zip).
- **ABS ASGS2025 Commonwealth Electoral Divisions**, GDA2020: official `CED_CODE25` / `CED_NAME25` and land-based geographic anchors. [Official archive](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/digital-boundary-files/CED_2025_AUST_GDA2020.zip). ABS CED geometries are Mesh Block approximations for statistical purposes, distinct from legal AEC boundaries.

All 150 classifications were cross-checked between official tables.
- **AEC national DivisionID/name/state identifiers**: [2025 official enrolment table](https://results.aec.gov.au/31496/Website/Downloads/GeneralEnrolmentByDivisionDownload-31496.csv). Codes are separate from ABS CED codes.
